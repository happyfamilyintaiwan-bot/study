12 weeks, 12 new pieces

One new piece each week; the next arrives 7 days later.

  1. 1
    WEEK 01・SafetyComing
    Front Door and Welcome Mat

    Open the door and lay down a welcome mat. This week you start the morning pages habit and give your creativity a safe place to begin.

  2. 2
    WEEK 02・IdentityComing
    Round Mirror

    Hang a round mirror and look at what you truly enjoy. This week you practice telling "I like" from "I should like".

  3. 3
    WEEK 03・PowerComing
    Big Window

    Open a big window and let the light in. Envy and frustration are clues that point to where you want to go.

  4. 4
    WEEK 04・IntegrityComing
    Tidy Bookshelf

    Set up a tidy bookshelf that holds only what you need. This week has a special challenge: seven days off reading, so your attention comes home to you.

  5. 5
    WEEK 05・PossibilityComing
    Starry Skylight

    A skylight full of stars opens in the roof. This week you let yourself dream before working out how.

  6. 6
    WEEK 06・AbundanceComing
    Plant Corner

    Bring in a corner full of green. Abundance does not need a big budget; this week you observe your relationship with money and time, and practice receiving.

  7. 7
    WEEK 07・ConnectionComing
    Record Player

    A record player brings sound into the studio. This week you practice listening closely, and starting before it is perfect.

  8. 8
    WEEK 08・StrengthComing
    Worktable

    Set down a solid worktable. Whatever your age, now is a fine time to begin.

  9. 9
    WEEK 09・CompassionComing
    Armchair and Throw

    Add a soft armchair. Be gentle with yourself this week; rest is part of making things.

  10. 10
    WEEK 10・Self-ProtectionComing
    Area Rug

    Lay down a rug that marks out your creative space. This week you look after your time and energy.

  11. 11
    WEEK 11・AutonomyComing
    Inspiration Shelf

    Put up a small shelf for the things that give you strength. This week you trust your own rhythm.

  12. 12
    WEEK 12・FaithComing
    Pendant Light

    Switch on the pendant light in the middle of the studio. Enjoy the making and leave the outcome to time.

04 / Guide

How to play

HOW TO PLAY
  1. Daily: write three morning pages by hand (or type here when you have no notebook). Your sprout grows along with you.
  2. Weekly: one artist date, one solo walk, the book's chapter for the week, and whichever of the four tasks speak to you.
  3. After 7 days: tap "Bring in" to add a new piece to the studio and open the next theme.
  4. After 12 weeks: the studio is full of your things and the pendant light comes on.

This is a creative habit practice. If you are carrying something heavy, talking with someone you trust or a professional can really help.

FAQ

FAQ

Is this an official The Artist's Way game?

No. Knitting Hiyori built this practice game around the book's core habits (morning pages, artist dates, solo walks, weekly chapters and tasks) and its 12-week structure. It is not affiliated with the author or publisher. The weekly prompts are our own; read the book for the full program.

Is my morning writing saved anywhere?

No. What you type stays in this tab and is cleared when you finish. It is never uploaded, never stored in your browser and never sent to analytics. The page only remembers which days you finished and which practices you checked, in your own browser.

Do I have to type here?

No, longhand is best. Keep a notebook, fill three pages, then tap "I wrote on paper" to finish the day. Typing here is a backup for days without your notebook.

Do I have to do every weekly task?

No. Each week has four tasks; do the ones that speak to you. The book also suggests choosing several from each week's list.

Can I finish all 12 weeks in a weekend?

Each new piece arrives 7 days after the last one. Taking it slowly is part of the practice.

What if I miss a few days?

Come back whenever you like; your progress is waiting. Your sprout waits too, and grows new leaves after a few days of writing.

What happens if I switch phones or clear my browser?

Progress lives in this browser, so a new device or cleared site data starts from the beginning.
